# Photo licences, stage 5 (November 24, 2026)

These are the four camera photographs added to the v7 desktop home page in stage 5. Each one was found through Openverse or Wikimedia Commons, looked at before it was chosen, cropped to 3:2 landscape and saved as webp at quality 80. The downloaded source files stay outside the repository in the session scratchpad.

The office photo is in advisory_people_800.webp and advisory_people_1600.webp. It comes from StockSnap at https://stocksnap.io/photo/business-people-IDYMLGDH3I, found through Openverse (record 3095e691-a957-4eb8-9460-d2090501ae3b). The author is Direct Media (https://stocksnap.io/author/145556) and the licence is CC0 1.0, public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. No attribution is required, and we credit the author anyway. The date taken is not given. It is a camera photograph: a StockSnap stock photo from a series of the same people and the same office shot from several angles, and Openverse had already indexed it on November 1, 2021. The copy we could reach carries no EXIF because StockSnap strips it. The only route open to this session served the photo at 960 by 640, so the 1600 file is 960 pixels wide, as the brief allows. The full 6720 pixel original is on the StockSnap page if a larger file is wanted later. Alt text: Five colleagues in business clothes meet around a white table with laptops in a bright modern office, while one of them talks with open hands.

The truck yard photo is in fleet_yard_camera_800.webp and fleet_yard_camera_1600.webp. It comes from Wikimedia Commons at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Colorful_Semi-Trucks.jpg, which is a copy of the Flickr photo https://www.flickr.com/photos/prayitnophotography/50738565482/. The author is listed on Commons under the Flickr name "Thank You (21 Millions+) views" (Flickr account prayitnophotography). The licence is CC BY 2.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/, so the author has to be credited. It was taken on December 18, 2020 at 09:27, at the Lebec rest area on Interstate 5 in California. It is a camera photograph: the EXIF shows a Canon EOS 77D at 53 mm, f/9, 1/500 second and ISO 200. We worked from the 1920 by 1280 rendition of the 5419 by 3612 original, which is already 3:2, so the full frame is kept. Alt text: A row of North American semi tractors, gold, red, white, blue and maroon, parked nose to tail with their trailers on a paved lot under dry brown hills and a clear blue sky.

The handshake photo is in handshake_800.webp and handshake_1600.webp. It comes from StockSnap at https://stocksnap.io/photo/shaking-hands-GEKK2UOHCY, found through Openverse (record 6a995f75-f409-4600-abe7-f0a2ce7d55d4). The author is Kristin Hardwick (https://www.kristinhardwick.com) and the licence is CC0 1.0, public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. The date taken is not given. It is a camera photograph: a StockSnap stock photo from a shoot by a named professional photographer, and the same shoot includes a second frame of the handshake shot closer in. Openverse indexed it on November 1, 2021, and the copy we could reach carries no EXIF. It was served at 960 by 640, so the 1600 file is 960 pixels wide. Alt text: A smiling woman in a white blazer shakes hands across an office desk with a woman in a mustard sweater, who is seen from behind, beside a coffee cup and a small plant.

The growth photo is in growth_road_800.webp and growth_road_1600.webp. It comes from Wikimedia Commons at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rest_Area,_Washington,_USA_(19153934089).jpg, which is a copy of the Flickr photo "Another Day, Another Dollar" at https://www.flickr.com/photos/yanush63/19153934089/. The author is Janusz Sobolewski, from Mississauga, Ontario, Canada. The licence is CC BY 2.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/, so the author has to be credited, and the Commons Flickr review bot confirmed the licence on May 3, 2026. It was taken on June 6, 2015 at 23:15, at a rest area in Washington state. The Commons file keeps no camera model. It is still a camera photograph: it was taken in 2015, years before photographic AI images existed, and it is one of the author's own Flickr photos in Commons categories for that rest area and that evening's sunset. We worked from the 1920 by 1203 rendition of the 5184 by 3248 original and cropped it to 1804 by 1203 at 3:2, centred on the truck. Alt text: A silver long nose semi truck with a bull bar and its lights on stands in an empty rest area lot under a pink, orange and deep blue sky just after sunset.
