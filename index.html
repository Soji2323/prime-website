Your dispatch system runs the loads. Who runs the file?

Your dispatch system moves your freight. It does not keep your driver files, your inspection records or your safety program. That work sits in a gap. A roadside officer or an auditor is often the first to find it.

We help your office close that gap. We are one thin layer: a Compliance & Safety Intelligence Platform. Our back office checks your file every week. Your office keeps the file. You see all of it on one screen. Each day, you can see what is in order and what is missing.

When it happens

Seven moments every owner knows.

A new driver starts on Monday.

You must vet each driver. The proof must stay on file for the day the government asks for it. A missing document can bring a fine. Repeated gaps can limit your right to operate.

In Greenlight your office person follows one list for each new driver. Every step and every document sits in that one place. You can see that the driver file is complete before the first trip.

A shipper asks for your carrier package.

It is the first thing a new shipper asks for. An old or untidy package can lose you the load. Greenlight makes your carrier package from your file in a few clicks. It carries your current documents.

Without a carrier package, a shipper most likely will not do business with you. A good carrier package takes a lot of time to put together. You must update the carrier package each time something changes.

With Greenlight, this takes a few clicks and costs nothing extra. Your office types less by hand, and fewer mistakes get in.

A date will expire soon.

Greenlight tracks the expiry date of each document. Your office gets a reminder before the date.

A driver gets a ticket.

A ticket or a violation raises your risk rating. That rating decides the quality of freight you can win. We go through it with you:

  • why it happened
  • what your options are
  • which habit stops it next time

The ticket may come from a scale near Calgary or a roadside check in British Columbia. The decision on what to do stays with you.

A letter comes from the government.

A letter from a regulator can be hard to read and harder to answer. We read it with your office. We guide the reply, so it is clear, correct and on time. Your office sends the reply.

The same guidance holds for each email your office sends to a government or regulatory body.

The regulator sets a date for your first audit.

The regulator usually audits a new carrier in its first year. This new carrier audit checks that your company meets the safety rules. The auditor looks at four things:

  • driver qualifications
  • maintenance and inspection schedules
  • hours of service
  • record keeping

Your office person prepares the file. We go through it with them before the day. Your office person meets the auditor with the file in order.

A driver needs to learn a rule.

Each lesson is a short video for the driver's phone, on one subject. Some drivers need more than a video. Your office sets up the call. We explain the rule in plain words.

We respect your company's internal dynamics. We offer insight to your drivers only when it helps. Then we talk with your drivers directly. We offer support and education so they are well informed and confident. Our goal is to help your business grow. We support everyone from management to the drivers on the road.

To start in Alberta and Canada

These are filings. You call us, and we file. We also help you with each renewal. Whether you run only in Alberta, across Canada or into the United States, it is the same.

To start in Alberta and Canada
  • Business incorporation (simple company structures only)
  • Safety Fitness Certificate (SFC) under the National Safety Code (NSC)
  • Workers' Compensation Board (WCB) registration
  • Prorate registration under the International Registration Plan (IRP)
  • Fuel tax licence under the International Fuel Tax Agreement (IFTA)
  • Drug testing account in Edmonton or Calgary

Prorate and the fuel tax licence help your office with fuel tax reports and cross border trips. We help your office meet National Safety Code (NSC) standards and get and keep the Safety Fitness Certificate (SFC). We help your office set up and manage drug test programs for Canadian and U.S. federal safety standards.

We guide your office through the WCB registration to meet Alberta legal requirements and support workplace safety. We help you incorporate the business. Incorporation gives limited liability, tax advantages and more credibility. A new trucking company does not have to be complicated to start. We help at every step with personalized advice and efficient setup services.

To run into the United States

To run into the United States
  • USDOT number
  • MC number
  • UCR registration
  • EIN for tax
  • SCAC code
  • Carrier Code
  • BOC-3 filing that names your process agents

If you plan to operate in the U.S., we file your USDOT and MC number applications. The applications help your fleet meet U.S. federal rules.

For cross border trucking, we file for your Carrier Code and file the BOC-3 for your process agents. The SCAC code supports efficient international freight movement.
